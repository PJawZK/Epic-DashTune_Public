Do not assume that Apache-2.0, GPL, MIT, or another open-source licence applies to this repository.

A future licence decision must:

1. establish which source and assets were created specifically for Epic DashTune;
2. identify inherited EpicDash/EpicEFI Virtual Dash material and its original terms;
3. record required copyright and attribution notices;
4. confirm that bundled artwork and generated assets may be redistributed;
5. separate dependency licences from the licence governing this repository's own source.

Until that audit is complete, contributions involving substantial copied code or third-party assets should not be submitted.
